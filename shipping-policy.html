Shipping Policy

We aim to make your THREAD & DIAL shopping experience simple and reliable. Please review our shipping information before placing an order.

1. Delivery Charges

A standard delivery charge of RS:200 is added to eligible orders at checkout.

2. Delivery Time

Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location, order processing time and courier service.

3. Order Processing

Orders are processed after the customer provides complete and accurate order information.

4. Delivery Address

Customers are responsible for providing a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number.

5. Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed due to courier issues, weather conditions, public holidays or other circumstances beyond our control.

6. Failed Delivery

If a delivery cannot be completed because the customer is unavailable or the provided information is incorrect, additional delivery arrangements may be required.

7. Order Support

If you have questions regarding your order or delivery, please contact THREAD & DIAL through WhatsApp or email.
