Return Policy

We want you to be satisfied with your THREAD & DIAL purchase. Please review the following return guidelines.

1. Return Eligibility

Products may be eligible for return if they are unused, unworn and in their original condition and packaging.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, contact THREAD & DIAL as soon as possible with your order details and relevant information about the issue.

3. Clothing Returns

Clothing should be returned unworn, unwashed and with original tags where applicable.

4. Watch Returns

Watches should be returned unused and in their original packaging with included accessories.

5. Return Shipping

Return shipping arrangements and charges may depend on the reason for the return and will be discussed with the customer before processing.

6. Refunds or Exchanges

After receiving and checking a returned item, we will communicate the available refund or exchange option with the customer.

7. Contact Us

For return-related questions, please contact THREAD & DIAL through WhatsApp or email available on our website.
